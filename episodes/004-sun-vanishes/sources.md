# Sources (simplified, for fun)
- Sunlight takes ~8 min 20 s to reach Earth (1 AU / c ≈ 499 s); changes in gravity also propagate at c.
- Moon is ~1.3 light-seconds away → goes dark ~1.3 s after the Sun's light stops reaching us.
- Jupiter is ~35–52 light-minutes from Earth: it would keep reflecting already-emitted sunlight for roughly 40 more minutes (light Sun→Jupiter ~43 min, Jupiter→Earth ~35–52 min).
- Earth's orbital speed ≈ 29.8 km/s → ≈ 2.6 million km/day in a straight line.
- Avg surface temperature ~below 0 °C within a week; ~−73 °C after a year (LiveScience, How It Works, Popular Science). Ice on top insulates the oceans; deep water stays liquid for a very long time; vent ecosystems run on Earth's internal heat.
