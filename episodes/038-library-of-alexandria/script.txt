[calm, intriguing documentary narrator, steady energy] What if the Library of Alexandria had never burned? [pause] Here's the twist: it never burned in one night. [building tension] It may have held forty thousand scrolls, or four hundred thousand. Nobody knows. Works we know existed are gone. Aristotle's dialogues. Eratosthenes' full map of the world. Aristarchus, who put the Sun at the centre, eighteen hundred years before Copernicus. [leaning in] And Alexandria already had a spinning steam engine. Could science have leapt a thousand years ahead?
