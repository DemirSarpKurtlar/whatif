[calm, intriguing documentary narrator, steady energy] What if Mount Fuji erupted today? [pause] Last time, in seventeen-oh-seven, ash fell for sixteen days. It reached Edo, a hundred kilometres away. Edo is now Tokyo. [building tension] Within about two hours, the sky over the capital turns grey. Half a millimetre of ash, and the trains stop. Three millimetres of wet ash on power lines, and the lights can go out. Ten centimetres, and ordinary cars can't move. That eruption threw out one point seven billion cubic metres of ash. Enough to fill Tokyo Dome nearly fourteen hundred times. [leaning in] Fuji has been silent for three hundred and nineteen years. Would you stay, or run?
