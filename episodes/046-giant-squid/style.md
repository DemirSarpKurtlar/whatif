# Style — "Woodblock Wave" (Higgsfield Content Factory: Japanese woodblock print · indigo sea · paper label plaques)

Same look as episode 041 (see `episodes/041-mount-fuji-erupts/style.md` for the full palette, line work and plaque rules).
The helpers come from 041's `src/lib.js` (paper print texture, bokashi skies, wave strokes, foam claws, great wave, cream double-border
plaques, indigo caption plaques); `src/build.py` inlines `src/lib.js` into every `draw/*.js` (edit src/, then `python3 src/build.py`).

## Additions for 046 (deep sea)
- Depth palette: indigo #1E3560 at the surface → prussian #101C38 → near-black abyss #070D1C; light fades with depth; marine-snow flecks.
- Giant squid printed in vermilion-brown (#A8402F / dark #6E2620 / light #D27556) with thick ink key lines, darker chromatophore dots,
  cream sucker rings; arms and tentacles as tapered woodblock strokes. Eye = concentric rings (silver-gold iris, ink pupil, cream highlight).
- Sperm whale = indigo-grey slab silhouette with cream key line and wrinkle hatching; sucker scars = cream rings.
- 2012 footage = a cream double-border "screen" plaque with corner brackets, REC dot and a golden squid in the dark.
- Kraken = hanging scroll (kakemono) with wooden rollers, print of a ship and arms rising from a curling sea.
- Red #B5302A only for key numbers (300 m, 27 cm, 13 m, 2012).
