[calm, intriguing documentary narrator, steady energy] What if you met a giant squid in the deep ocean? [pause] You sink past three hundred metres, and the sunlight is gone. [building tension] Then something looks back. An eye twenty-seven centimetres wide, the size of a dinner plate. The biggest eyes of any animal, along with the colossal squid. From fin to tentacle tip, up to thirteen metres. As long as a city bus. Nobody filmed one alive down here until twenty-twelve, off Japan. [leaning in] Its only real enemy? Sperm whales, covered in its sucker scars. It may be the monster sailors called the Kraken. Would you stay and watch, or swim for the surface?
