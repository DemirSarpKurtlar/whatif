# Sources / numbers (simplified, for fun)
- Radial free fall from 1 AU into the Sun: t = (π/2)·√(R³/2GM) ≈ 64.6 days (≈ 65 days; Universe Today; Physics Forums gives 63-66 days).
  https://www.universetoday.com/articles/earth-stopped-orbiting-sun
  https://www.physicsforums.com/threads/earth-reaches-suns-chromosphere-timing-estimate.528653/post-3494188
- Fall timeline computed with the radial Kepler (cycloid) solution: after 7 days r ≈ 0.993 AU (≈1 million km fallen); Venus orbit (0.723 AU) ≈ day 41;
  r = 0.6 AU ≈ day 48; Mercury orbit (0.387 AU) ≈ day 57; 0.1 AU ≈ day 63.7.
- Apparent Sun diameter ∝ 1/r: 1.4× at Venus' orbit, 2.6× at Mercury's.
- Temperature: crude scaling T ≈ 288 K·r^-1/2 → ≈372 K (≈100 °C) near 0.6 AU (ignores runaway greenhouse/steam effects).
- Orbital speed of Earth ≈ 29.8 km/s (107,000 km/h). Impact speed at the solar surface ≈ √(2GM(1/R☉ − 1/AU)) ≈ 616 km/s.
- Tidal disruption near the Roche limit would break Earth apart before impact (noted on end card).
