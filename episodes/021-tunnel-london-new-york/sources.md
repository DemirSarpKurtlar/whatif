# Sources / math — 021 London–New York gravity tunnel (Tunnel Part 2)
- "Gravity train": a frictionless, airless straight tunnel through a uniform-density Earth gives simple harmonic motion with period 2π√(R/g) ≈ 84.4 min, so any chord takes ≈ 42.2 min one way, regardless of length. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity_train
- London–New York great-circle distance ≈ 5,570 km → central angle 0.874 rad → chord ≈ 5,394 km.
- Deepest point: R(1 − cos(θ/2)) ≈ 599 km below the surface.
- Top speed: √(g/R) · R · sin(θ/2) ≈ 3.35 km/s ≈ 12,000 km/h.
- Simplifications: uniform-density Earth, no air, no friction, Earth's rotation ignored (real Earth's dense core shortens the through-centre trip to ~38 min).
