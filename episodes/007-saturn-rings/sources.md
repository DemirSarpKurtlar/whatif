# Sources (Saturn's rings scaled to Earth)
- Saturn equatorial radius ≈ 60,268 km; main rings from C ring inner edge ≈ 74,500 km (1.24 Rs) to A ring outer edge ≈ 136,775 km (2.27 Rs); Cassini Division ≈ 117,580–122,170 km (1.95–2.03 Rs). Britannica; NASA ring fact sheets.
  https://www.britannica.com/place/Saturn-planet/The-ring-system
- Scaled to Earth (R = 6,371 km): inner edge ≈ 7,900 km from centre (≈1,500 km altitude), outer edge ≈ 14,460 km (≈8,100 km altitude).
- Main-ring thickness typically ~10 m (up to tens of metres).
- Geometry (computed): from latitude 40.7°N the outer edge stands ~26° above the southern horizon; rings are invisible beyond latitude arccos(1/2.27) ≈ 64°.
- At the equator the ring plane passes through the zenith → seen edge-on as a line.
- Ring shadow falls on the winter hemisphere (sun on the other side of the ring plane); ringlight brightens nights.
