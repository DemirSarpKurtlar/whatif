[calm, intriguing documentary narrator, steady energy] What if you fell into an active volcano? Forget the movies. You wouldn't sink. [pause] Lava is molten rock, nearly three times denser than your body. So you'd land on top, on a thin black crust. [building tension] But the crater gets you first. Sulfur dioxide burns your throat. Carbon dioxide pools low, and a few breaths can knock you out. Under the crust, cracks glow at eleven hundred degrees. That heat is eighty times what your skin can stand. [leaning in] At Nyiragongo, the lava lake sits hundreds of metres below the rim. At Erta Ale, most of the lake is crust. Would you look over the edge?
