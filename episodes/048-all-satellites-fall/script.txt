[tense, gripping documentary narrator, steady energy] What if every satellite fell out of the sky? Tonight, about seventeen thousand working satellites start dropping. [pause] Most burn up as shooting stars. But the space station weighs four hundred tonnes, and big chunks could hit the ground. [building tension] Then GPS goes dark. Planes, ships and phone maps get lost. Cell towers, power grids and banks use GPS as a clock, so they switch to backups. In the US, losing GPS could cost a billion dollars a day. Weather forecasts lose ninety percent of their data, so hurricanes hit with less warning. [leaning in] Building them took almost seventy years. What would you miss first?
