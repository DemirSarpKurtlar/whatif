# Sources — What if every satellite fell out of the sky?

The scenario is hypothetical (in reality GEO satellites would stay up for over a million years and GPS for ~100,000+ years; "falling" is the premise).
The numbers below are real and were kept conservative where sources disagree.
(Note: planet4589.org — Jonathan McDowell's own catalogue — could not be fetched from this sandbox; his counts were taken from outlets quoting him.)
