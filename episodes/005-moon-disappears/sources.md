# Sources / numbers (simplified)
- Mean Earth–Moon distance 384,400 km → light travel time ≈ 1.28 s (NASA Moon fact sheet).
- Full Moon illuminance ≈ 0.1–0.3 lux; clear moonless starlight ≈ 0.001–0.002 lux → full Moon ≈ 100×+ brighter.
- Solar tidal force ≈ 46% of lunar; with lunar tide gone the range is ≈ 0.46/1.46 ≈ ⅓ of today's (NOAA Tides & Currents).
- Mass coral spawning (e.g. Great Barrier Reef) is cued by lunar phase/moonlight (Kaniewska et al. 2015; Lin et al. 2021).
- Earth's obliquity ≈ 23.4°, stabilised by the Moon; without it, chaotic variation up to ~0–85° over millions of years (Laskar, Joutel & Robutel 1993, Nature).
