# Sources / numbers (simplified, for fun)
- Earth's field is generated by convecting molten iron in the outer core (geodynamo); surface field ≈ 25-65 µT.
- Dayside magnetopause sits ≈ 10 Earth radii out (≈ 64,000 km) toward the Sun (NASA).
- Solar wind speed ≈ 400 km/s ≈ 1.4-1.5 million km/h (NASA).
- Laschamp excursion (~41,000 years ago): field fell to a few % and auroras appeared at low latitudes. https://livescience.com/wandering-aurora-laschamp-event
- Satellites in orbit: >10,000 active (2025-2026 estimates).
- Birds, sea turtles and salmon use magnetoreception to navigate.
- Mars lost its global field ~4 billion years ago; solar wind stripping (MAVEN) removed much of its atmosphere; on Earth, loss would be slow (millions of years). https://medium.com/@deep.space/what-would-happen-if-earths-magnetic-field-turned-off-for-one-day-d4730ef56e05 (informal)
- Simplification: the real field cannot switch off overnight; it weakens over centuries during reversals.
