# Sources / numbers (simplified, for fun)
- Moon distance 384,400 km; Jupiter equatorial diameter 142,984 km → angular size 2·atan(71,492/384,400) ≈ 21° vs the Moon's 0.52° → ~40× wider. A hand at arm's length spans ~20°.
- Great Red Spot ≈ 16,000 km wide (≈1.3 Earth diameters, 2020s).
- Jupiter mass = 318 Earth masses → Earth would orbit Jupiter. Period at a = 384,400 km: T = 2π√(a³/GM_J) ≈ 1.33×10⁵ s ≈ 37 h.
- Tidal acceleration ∝ M/d³ → Jupiter/Moon mass ratio ≈ 25,800 → ~26,000× the Moon's tidal pull (at equal distance).
- Brightness: ~1,600× the Moon's disc area × albedo ratio (~0.5/0.12) ≈ thousands of times a full Moon (~7,000×, rough).
- Io (orbit 421,700 km) is the most volcanically active body in the Solar System, driven by Jupiter's tidal heating. Roche limit for Earth around Jupiter ≈ 2.44·R_J·(ρ_J/ρ_E)^(1/3) ≈ 110,000 km, so Earth survives at 384,400 km.
