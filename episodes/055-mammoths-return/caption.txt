What if woolly mammoths came back? 🦣❄️

The last mammoths lived on Wrangel Island until ~4,000 years ago, after the pyramids were built. Colossal Biosciences now aims for 150+ gene edits to Asian elephant cells, but an elephant pregnancy lasts up to 22 months, so the first mammoth-like calf is expected in the early 2030s. In Siberia's Pleistocene Park, trampling herds already cooled the soil ~1.9 °C.
