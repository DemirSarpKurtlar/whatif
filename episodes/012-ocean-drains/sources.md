# Sources / numbers (simplified, for fun)
- Ocean volume ≈ 1.33 billion km³ (NOAA); ≈ 97 % of Earth's water.
- Average ocean depth ≈ 3,700 m (NOAA).
- RMS Titanic wreck ≈ 3,800 m deep (≈12,500 ft), North Atlantic.
- Mid-ocean ridge ≈ 65,000 km long — the longest mountain range on Earth (NOAA / USGS; some estimates differ by measurement). https://www.scienceiq.com/facts/UnderseaEarthsGeography.cfm
- Challenger Deep, Mariana Trench ≈ 10,935 m (estimates 10,920-11,035 m). https://seawifs.gsfc.nasa.gov/OCEAN_PLANET/HTML/oceanography_plunge.html
- Mount Everest 8,849 m → about 2 km of room to spare.
- Marine phytoplankton produce roughly half of Earth's oxygen (NOAA).
