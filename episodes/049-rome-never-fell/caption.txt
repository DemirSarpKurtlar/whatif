What if the Roman Empire never fell? 🏛️⚙️✨

At its peak: maybe 60 million people, a fifth of humanity, linked by 400,000 km of roads. The Pantheon's 43.3 m concrete dome still stands, unreinforced, after 1,900 years. They had a gear computer that predicted eclipses and a spinning steam engine 1,600 years before a useful one.

Instead, Rome shrank from a million people to maybe 30,000. Would you live in a Rome that never fell? 👇
