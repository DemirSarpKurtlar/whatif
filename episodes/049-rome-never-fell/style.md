# Style — "Ink & Gold" (Higgsfield Content Factory: sumi-ink wash · silhouettes · gold-leaf accents)

Same look as episode 038 (see episodes/038-library-of-alexandria/style.md for the reference study). We adapt the LOOK only — no story, wording,
characters or shots from the reference video. The draw approach (rice-paper texture, blurred ink washes, bristle brush strokes, gold-leaf pattern,
silhouettes, paper-grain finish pass) is copied from 038's draw helpers into every draw/*.js file of this episode, plus new Roman silhouettes.

## Palette
- Rice paper greys: #D6D0C2 / #9C978E / #55524D, near-black ink #141312.
- Gold leaf #8C6A1E → #C9A64B → #F2D68A for the focal object only (sun disc, lit arches, roads, gears, sunbeam, boiler); one red seal stamp (#B8322A).

## New silhouettes for Rome (draw helpers in every file)
- `colosseum()` — 18 foreshortened bays × 4 tiers, arch openings glowing gold when backlit; optional famous broken profile and a "crumble" parameter.
- `arcade()` — two-tier aqueduct arches receding in perspective, gold water glints running along the channel.
- `gear()` — toothed gold-leaf gears with spoke windows (Antikythera); `domeBldg()`, `pine()`, `cypress()` for skylines.

## Scenes (one focal gold element each)
1. Rome at its height: gold sun behind the lit Colosseum, gold road, walkers. 2. 476: temple splits on a gold fissure, half topples.
3. Crowd rows filling a forum, every fifth figure turns gold. 4. Ink map of the Mediterranean, gold roads spreading from Roma.
5. Aqueduct arcades converging on the city. 6. Pantheon section: coffered dome, gold sunbeam, dashed 43.3 m sphere.
7. Corroded bronze lump → turning gold gears + eclipse dial. 8. Hero's aeolipile spinning over fire.
9. Broken Colosseum, sheep, gold city lights going out. 10. SPECULATION: gold steam train on an aqueduct viaduct, "?" seal.
11. Colosseum crumbling into gold dust. 12. SPECULATION: Rome today — towers with pediments, a dome, gold maglev on arches, a robed figure watching.

## Type + pacing
- Engine serif overlay (LiberationSerif), accent gold #F2D68A for the spoken word and HUD values; captions over dark ink bands (y≈1180-1500).
- Cuts every 2-4 s with 0.3 s dissolves; slow push-ins; one shake on the 476 split.
