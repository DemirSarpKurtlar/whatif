# Sources / numbers (simplified, for fun)
- Earth's rotation speed at the equator ≈ 1,670 km/h; at New York's latitude (40.7°) ≈ 1,670·cos(40.7°) ≈ 1,266 km/h.
- With no gravity, a person moves in a straight line while the ground keeps rotating: outward drift a = ω²·r = (7.29e-5)²·4.83e6 m ≈ 0.026 m/s² → ≈0.12 m after 3 s, ≈1.3 m after 10 s.
- Airliners in cruise produce lift ≈ weight; with weight gone, net upward accel ≈ 9.8 m/s² → ½·9.8·10² ≈ 490 m in 10 s (before pilots react).
- Sun's gravitational acceleration at 1 AU ≈ 0.0059 m/s² → missing it for 10 s ≈ ½·0.0059·100 ≈ 0.30 m deviation.
- Earth's orbit length ≈ 2π·1 AU ≈ 940 million km.
- Atmosphere & interior decompression with gravity removed: qualitative (pressure no longer balanced by weight).
