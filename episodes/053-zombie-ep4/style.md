# Style — EP 4 (Zombie Outbreak series look)
Same fixed series look as EP1–EP3: flat 2D vector "night flat"-inspired cartoon (engine `style: "flat"`, draw scenes on
engine/draw/flatlib.js). Rounded shapes, no outlines, limited palette: navy/violet town, EP3's dawn gradient
(violet → pink → orange) warming into a brighter morning sky as the ride goes on; zombies in soft green with red eyes;
the protagonist in red beanie + purple hoodie + red sneakers, now on the neighbor's red bike.
Adapted only the look (flat geometry, palette, chunky LHeavy type with dark outline, pill labels), never story or shots
from any reference. New pieces drawn in the same vocabulary: side-scrolling parallax ride, top HUD cards
(distance left · time · infected), comic "CLICK"/"RIIIP!" pops, soldiers in olive helmets, chain-link stadium gate.
End card: series flat end card with A/B choices.
