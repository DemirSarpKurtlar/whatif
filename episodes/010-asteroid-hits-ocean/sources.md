# Sources / numbers (simplified; impact-tsunami results are model-dependent)
- Ward & Asphaug (2003), "Asteroid impact tsunami of 2880 March 16" (asteroid 1950 DA, ~1.1 km, 17.8 km/s): ~60,000 Mt; transient cavity 19 km wide, as deep as the ocean (~5 km); tsunami "hundreds of metres" near the impact; ~120 m (400 ft) waves reaching the US East Coast about 2 hours later. https://news.ucsc.edu/2003/05/massive-tsunami-sweeps-atlantic-coast-in-asteroid-impact-scenario-for-march-16-2880/ , https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2003/05/030528081215.htm
- Hiroshima bomb ≈ 15 kt → 60,000 Mt / 0.015 Mt ≈ 4 million.
- Deep-ocean tsunami speed √(g·h) ≈ √(9.8·4500) ≈ 210 m/s ≈ 750 km/h (jet-airliner speed).
- Horizon drop at 600 km ≈ d²/2R ≈ 28 km, so a stratospheric plume (> 30 km) would rise above the horizon.
- Sea retreats before many tsunamis arrive (trough first) — shown as the beach drawdown.
