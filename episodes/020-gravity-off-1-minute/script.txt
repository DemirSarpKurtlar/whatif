[calm, intriguing documentary narrator, steady energy] What if gravity switched off for one full minute? Last time, ten seconds was harmless. You floated up a metre and landed fine. [pause] But the longer it lasts, the faster the ground falls away. Earth keeps spinning. You fly in a straight line. [building tension] Ten seconds: one metre. Thirty seconds: nine metres. Sixty seconds: thirty-five metres. That's twelve storeys up. Above you, the air is drifting off into space. Every plane is still climbing. [pause] Then gravity snaps back. [leaning in] From twelve storeys, you hit the ground at ninety-five kilometres an hour. Ten seconds was a ride. One minute is a fall. Where would you grab on?
