# Sources / math — 020 gravity off for 1 minute (Part 2)
- Surface rotation speed at New York latitude (40.7°): 465 m/s × cos(40.7°) ≈ 352 m/s ≈ 1,266 km/h (same assumption as episode 001).
- With no gravity you keep moving in a straight line while the ground rotates; height above ground after t seconds h ≈ (v·t)² / (2R), R = 6,371 km:
  10 s → 0.97 m · 30 s → 8.8 m · 60 s → 35 m (≈ 12 storeys at ~3 m/storey).
- Impact speed when gravity returns at 35 m: v = √(2·9.81·35) ≈ 26 m/s ≈ 94 km/h.
- Planes in level flight make lift ≈ weight, so with weight gone they accelerate upward (as in episode 001).
- Atmosphere: no longer held down, the top of the atmosphere expands into space; the expansion travels down at roughly the speed of sound, so air near the ground stays breathable for minutes.
