# Style — "Woodblock Wave" (Higgsfield Content Factory: Japanese woodblock print · indigo sea · paper label plaques)

Same look as episodes 041 and 046 (see `episodes/041-mount-fuji-erupts/style.md` for palette, line work and plaque rules).
Helpers are copied from 046's `src/lib.js` (paper print texture, bokashi skies, wave strokes, foam claws, great wave, cream double-border
plaques, indigo caption plaques, squid, diver, depth gradients); `src/build.py` inlines `src/lib.js` into every `draw/*.js`
(edit src/, then `python3 src/build.py`). We adapt the LOOK only; story, wording and shots are our own.

## Additions for 050 (whales)
- Three whale prints, all side views with thick ink key lines and wrinkle / pleat hatching, cream rim line on dark water:
  - blue whale: long slim mottled steel-blue body (#6F8FB3 / dark #4A6688, cream mottles), tiny dorsal fin, throat pleats;
  - sperm whale: indigo-grey block head (#3A4866), narrow lower jaw that swings open with cream teeth, pink-cream mouth;
  - humpback: near-black indigo (#232C44) with long cream pectoral fins, head knobs, cream throat pleats, pouch that bulges.
- Throat chart = cream paper diagram panel (like 046's size chart): throat ring vs beach ball vs a person's shoulders, red dimension bars.
- Stomach = anatomical woodblock print: vermilion muscular sacs with ink rugae folds on a maroon body cavity (#2A1418);
  chamber 1 walls pulse (squeeze), chamber 2 is a yellow-green acid pool (#B9B54A) with cream bubbles; numbered cream seal plaques.
- Cape Cod 2021 = bokashi sky, low sand dunes and a tiny Provincetown tower on the horizon, humpback lunging out of the indigo sea,
  an "x-ray" cream dashed window showing the diver inside its closed mouth, a 0→40 s counter plaque.
- Red #B5302A only for key numbers/words (20 cm, 40 cm, 10 m, 4, 2021, 40 s, ACID).
