What if a whale swallowed you? 🐋😱

A blue whale can't: its throat is only about 20 cm wide, smaller than a beach ball. A sperm whale could, it swallows squid whole, into 4 stomach chambers: one crushes, the next is full of acid. In 2021, a humpback held diver Michael Packard in its mouth for up to 40 seconds, then spat him out alive.

Would you rather face the squeeze, or the acid? 👇
