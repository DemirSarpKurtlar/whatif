What if Yellowstone's supervolcano erupted? 🌋🇺🇸

2.1 million years ago one blast threw out about 2,450 km³ of ash. A super-eruption today could drop centimetres of ash as far as Minneapolis and cool the planet by a few degrees.
But the magma below is only 5-15% melt, and the yearly odds are about 1 in 730,000 (USGS). A lava flow or steam blast is far more likely.
