# Style — "Woodblock Wave", Yellowstone edition (as 041 Mount Fuji, which performed well)

Same LOOK as episode 041 (ukiyo-e woodblock print): the shared helpers in `src/lib.js` are copied from
`episodes/041-mount-fuji-erupts/src/lib.js` (paper multiply texture, bokashi skies, key-lined ridges, cream kasumi
mist bands, two-tone billow plume, cream double-bordered serif plaques with vermilion numbers, indigo caption plaques).
Nothing of 041's story, wording or shots is reused; this episode is about the Yellowstone caldera.

## Adaptations for Yellowstone
- Landscape: Absaroka-style ridges in steel blue with cream snow drips, wide sage/ochre caldera plain instead of a bay,
  dense dark lodgepole pine fringes (tall narrow spires, printed in two greens), Yellowstone Lake as an indigo band with
  cream wave strokes.
- Thermal features: Old Faithful drawn as a woodblock geyser (white column of stacked cream billows + steam puffs),
  Grand Prismatic-style hot spring as concentric printed rings (blue core, cream, ochre, rust edge).
- Bison: flat ink-brown silhouettes with a darker hump/mane block and a cream key highlight, slow grazing bob.
- Super-eruption: the 041 billow plume scaled up and rising from a ring of vents along the caldera rim (several columns
  merging into one umbrella cloud), ash fall flecks.
- Diagrams in print style: woodblock map of the US (cream land, indigo seas, compass rose) with ash zones as stippled
  grey bull's-eye bands; timeline scroll; volume cubes; cut-away cross-section of the crust with a "crystal mush"
  reservoir (cream crystals packed with a little red melt between them); a thermometer plaque; a field of dots for the odds.
- Palette additions: sage #9AA57A, ochre #C8A25A, rust #A8582F, bison #4A3426 / #2E2018, magma red #B5302A / orange #E07A3A.

## Camera + pacing
Slow push-ins and pans, a mist band crossing at cuts, a new plaque/number every 2-4 s. Cold open: the eruption shot.
