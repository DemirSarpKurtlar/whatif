# Sources / notes (simplified, for fun)
- Walking relies on static friction between sole and ground (μ ≈ 0.4-0.8); with μ = 0 the first push makes the foot slide.
- Nails and screws hold by friction (nails: friction from wood compression; screws: thread friction prevents back-out under vibration/load).
- Knots and woven fabric hold by fiber-to-fiber friction (capstan effect).
- Root anchorage relies largely on soil-root friction/shear.
- Newton's first law: a rolling ball keeps going without rolling resistance or air drag.
