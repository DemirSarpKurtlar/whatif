# Sources / numbers (simplified, for fun)
- ~1,350 potentially active volcanoes worldwide on land (USGS Volcano Hazards Program, "about 1,350 potentially active volcanoes").
- Plinian ash columns of large eruptions reach 20-40+ km (Pinatubo 1991 ~35 km; Tambora 1815 ~43 km). Airliners cruise ~10-12 km.
- Volcanic lightning: charge separation from ash/ice particle collisions in the plume.
- Eyjafjallajökull, Iceland, April 2010: ~100,000 flights cancelled over ~8 days (Eurocontrol), ~10 million passengers affected.
- Stratospheric sulfate aerosols reflect sunlight; Tambora 1815 (VEI 7) → 1816 "Year Without a Summer", global cooling ≈ 0.4-0.7 °C, June snow in New England, crop failures in Europe/N. America (sources: IOP 2019 study; Guinness; Big Think).
