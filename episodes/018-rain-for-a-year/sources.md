# Sources / numbers (simplified)
- Global mean precipitation ≈ 990-1,100 mm/yr; ≈ 505,000 km³ of water per year (Earth rainfall climatology; Britannica "World distribution of precipitation").
- 1 mm/h × 8,760 h = 8.76 m in a year ≈ 9× the global average.
- Atacama: some stations average < 1 mm/yr; rare rains trigger the "desierto florido" bloom from long-dormant seeds.
- Persistent cloud cover cuts surface sunlight (cooler days), saturated soils trigger landslides and floods.
