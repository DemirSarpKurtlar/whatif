[calm, intriguing documentary narrator, steady energy] What if you fell into a black hole? Pick a small one, ten times the mass of the Sun. Almost four thousand kilometres out, gravity pulls your feet much harder than your head. [building tension] Your body is stretched like spaghetti. The last stretch to the edge takes less than a quarter of a second. [pause] Now pick a giant one, four million Suns, like the one at the centre of our galaxy. You cross the edge and feel nothing at all. [leaning in] But your friends, watching from far away, never see you fall in. You freeze, fade red, and vanish. Which one would you pick?
