# Sources / math — 022 falling into a black hole
- Schwarzschild radius r_s = 2GM/c²: 10 M☉ → 29.5 km (≈ 60 km across); Sgr A* (4.3 million M☉) → 12.7 million km (≈ 25 million km across). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwarzschild_radius · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagittarius_A*
- Tidal stretch over a 2 m body: Δa ≈ 4GM·h/r³ → for 10 M☉, Δa = 10 g at r ≈ 3,800 km. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaghettification
- Free-fall time from 3,800 km to the horizon (Newtonian): ≈ 0.22 s from rest, less if already falling.
- Sgr A*: tidal difference at the horizon ≈ 0.0001 g → nothing felt.
- Distant observers see an infalling object slow down, redshift and fade, never quite crossing the horizon. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Event_horizon
