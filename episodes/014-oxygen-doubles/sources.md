# Sources / notes (simplified, for fun)
- Air is ~20.9% O2 by volume. Doubling oxygen by adding O2 raises total pressure by ~21% (1 → ~1.21 atm).
- Short exposures to ~40% O2 at ~1 atm are harmless; pulmonary oxygen toxicity needs hours to days.
- Flammability rises steeply with O2: below ~15% wildfires can't spread; above ~25% even damp vegetation can burn (Watson & Lovelock; Glasspool & Scott 2010, Nature Geoscience). https://dx.doi.org/10.1146%2Fannurev.earth.31.100901.141329
- Carboniferous O2 estimated ~35% (Berner model); Meganeura griffenflies had ~65-70 cm wingspans. https://www.discovermagazine.com/insects-of-the-oxygeniferous-11783
- Fires already burning keep going once O2 returns to 21%.
