# Sources / numbers (simplified)
- ~10 quintillion (10^19) insects alive at any time — Smithsonian BugInfo "Numbers of Insects" (https://www.si.edu/spotlight/buginfo/bugnos). ÷ 8.2 billion people ≈ 1.2 billion each.
- 87 of the 115 leading global food crops (~75%) benefit from animal pollination — Klein et al. 2007, Proc. R. Soc. B (also cited by IPBES 2016 pollinator assessment).
- Most terrestrial songbirds feed insects to nestlings; insectivores include frogs, lizards, bats, many freshwater fish.
- Insects (dung beetles, termites, fly larvae, etc.) are major decomposers/nutrient recyclers.
- Rice and wheat are self-pollinating, maize wind-pollinated — staple grains do not need insects.
