# Sources — What if you stood on the Sun for 1 second?

| Claim in script | Value used | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The Sun has no solid surface; it is plasma | "nothing to stand on", "The Sun is plasma" | NASA JPL ST5 "The Sun": https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/nmp/st5/SCIENCE/sun.html ("doesn't have a solid surface where we could stand"); NASA SOHO Explore: https://soho.nascom.nasa.gov/explore ("neither a solid nor a gas but is actually plasma") |
| Photosphere is thousands of times thinner than air | ~2 × 10⁻⁴ kg/m³ ≈ 1/6,000 of sea-level air (1.2 kg/m³) | Montana State Univ. Solar Physics, "The Solar Atmosphere": https://solar.physics.montana.edu/hurlbutt/Atmosphere.html ; OpenStax Astronomy "Structure and Composition of the Sun": https://brianhill.github.io/physics90/resources_unit_3/StructureAndCompositionOfTheSunFromOpenStax.pdf |
| Surface gravity 28 × Earth | 274 m/s² ÷ 9.81 m/s² = 27.9 | NASA Sun Fact Sheet (NSSDC): https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/sunfact.html ; Radio JOVE (NASA GSFC) Sun facts: https://radiojove.gsfc.nasa.gov/education/sun/basics/material/sunfacts.htm |
| You would weigh over two tonnes | 80 kg × 27.9 ≈ 2,230 kg-force | derived from the 274 m/s² figure above |
| Surface temperature 5,500 °C | effective temperature 5,772 K ≈ 5,500 °C | NASA Sun Fact Sheet (NSSDC), link above |
| 63 megawatts per square metre | σT⁴ = 5.67×10⁻⁸ × 5,772⁴ ≈ 6.3×10⁷ W/m² (surface radiant flux) | Stefan–Boltzmann law with the NSSDC effective temperature; cf. NASA fact sheet "surface emission" ~ 6.3×10⁷ W/m² |
| "like sixty thousand microwave ovens" | typical microwave oven ≈ 1,000 W cooking power → 63,000 ovens per m² | derived |
| Skin boils away in ~a tenth of a second | energy to heat + vaporise water ≈ 2.6 MJ/kg; outer ~2 mm of skin ≈ 2 kg/m² → ~5 MJ/m² ÷ 63 MJ/s·m² ≈ 0.08 s (order-of-magnitude estimate, ignores reflection) | derived; latent heat of vaporisation of water 2.26 MJ/kg (standard value) |
| Light/energy may spend ~100,000 years escaping the core | published estimates of the random-walk escape time range from ~10,000 to ~170,000 years (some textbooks quote much longer) — script says "may have spent a hundred thousand years" | Futurism, "A Photon's Journey Through the Sun": https://futurism.com/a-photons-journey-through-the-sun ; Wikipedia "Sun" (Core section, 10,000–170,000 yr range) |

Notes
- Title says "1 second"; in one second of free fall at 274 m/s² you would drop ~137 m (not used in narration, kept for HUD only if needed).
- No claim is made about surviving; all numbers are rounded the way they are spoken.
