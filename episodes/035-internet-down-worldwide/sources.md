# Sources — What if the internet went down worldwide for 24 hours?

Scenario is hypothetical; anchors below are real. Wording in the script was kept conservative where sources disagree.

| Claim in script / on screen | Source(s) | Note |
|---|---|---|

| "grounded thousands of flights" | [Wikipedia: 2024 CrowdStrike incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_CrowdStrike-related_IT_outages) (~5,078 flights cancelled on the day, 4.6% of scheduled); [InvGate stats](https://blog.invgate.com/crowdstrike-it-outage-stats) (FlightAware: 12,309 cancelled through 22 July) | Totals vary by source; "thousands" is safe |
| CrowdStrike cost Fortune 500 ~$5.4B (on-screen only, optional) | [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/07/24/fortune-500-crowdstrike-outage-impact), [Parametrix report](https://parametrixinsurance.com/reports-white-papers/crowdstrikes-impact-on-the-fortune-500) | Insurer model estimate |
| Hospitals switch to paper | [Healthcare IT News](https://www.healthcareitnews.com/news/worldwide-it-outage-disrupts-healthcare-delivery), [PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/connectedeconomy/2024/hospitals-cancel-procedures-find-manual-workarounds-amid-microsoft-outage), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/07/19/crowdstrike-outage-glitch-hospitals-healthcare-doctors-computer-systems/) | During CrowdStrike, hospitals used "downtime protocol, paper orders"; NHS GPs used paper records |
