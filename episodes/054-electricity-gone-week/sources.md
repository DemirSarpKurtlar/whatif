# Sources — 054 What if electricity disappeared for one week?

Scenario: every power plant, line and battery-free grid source stops for 7 days. Each "day" beat uses a real, documented
outage effect. Numbers are rounded as spoken in the narration.

## Day 1 — cell towers run on batteries for ~4-8 hours
- FCC (2007 post-Katrina order) set a benchmark of 8 hours of battery backup at cell sites (24 h at switching centers); carriers fought it in court.
  https://www.heraldnet.com/news/fcc-cell-companies-clash-over-rules-on-towers-backup-power
- Typical tower batteries: 2-8 hours; batteries "up to 4-8 hours", generators (where installed) 24-72 h.
  https://www.fuellogic.net/cell-tower-backup-power/
  https://www.energy-storage.news/texas-winter-storm-highlights-the-importance-of-battery-storage-for-telecom-networks/
- Fairfax County: backup power for phone service in outages.
  https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/cableconsumer/cable/backup-power

## Day 2 — water pressure fails (pumps need power); Texas 2021: ~15 million told to boil water
- Feb 2021 Texas winter storm: pump failures + power loss dropped pressure; at the peak nearly 15 million Texans (about half the state) faced water disruptions / boil-water notices.
  https://www.npr.org/sections/live-updates-winter-storms-2021/2021/02/17/968887365/millions-in-texas-under-boil-water-notices-because-of-winter-storm
  https://api.texastribune.org/2021/02/19/texas-water-power-outages/
  https://www.foxnews.com/us/14m-texas-residents-still-under-boil-water-notices-as-tens-of-thousands-lack-running-water.print
- 2003 Northeast blackout: Detroit's water system lost pressure; boil-water alert for ~4.3 million customers; Cleveland got water by National Guard tanker.
  https://www.wwdmag.com/contaminants/detroit-water-safe-drink
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeast_blackout_of_2003  (~55 million people affected)

## Day 3 — full freezer holds ~48 hours, fridge ~4 hours
- FoodSafety.gov: refrigerator keeps food cold ~4 h if unopened; a full freezer holds temperature ~48 h (24 h if half full).
  https://www.foodsafety.gov/blog/poweroutage.html
  https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/emergencies/keep-your-food-safe-during-emergencies

## Day 4 — 96 hours: hospitals must plan to sustain themselves for up to 96 h
- Joint Commission Emergency Management standard: hospitals must plan how they would sustain operations for up to 96 hours without outside support (fuel, water, supplies). NFPA 110 "Class 96" = 96 h of generator fuel (required in some cases / jurisdictions).
  https://jointcommission.org/standards/standard-faqs/home-care/environment-of-care-ec/000001246
  https://www.csemag.com/articles/hospital-emergency-power-supply-systems/
  https://hfmmagazine.com/node/696787

## Day 5 — gas pumps are electric
- Hurricane Irma 2017: most Florida gas stations had no backup generation, so pumps could not work; Sandy 2012: "full tanks and no power".
  https://www.npr.org/2017/09/12/550464023/with-power-out-many-florida-gas-stations-remain-closed
  https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/local/sandy-long-gas-lines-station-pumps-new-jersey-fuel-shortage/1966715/

## Day 6 — no cards, no ATMs, cash only
- Spain/Portugal blackout, 28 April 2025: card terminals and ATMs failed, shops went cash-only or closed; card spending fell 41%.
  https://www.bde.es/wbe/en/noticias-eventos/blog/como-pagamos-cuando-todo-se-apaga-la-experiencia-del-apagon-en-espana.html
  https://www.bbvaresearch.com/en/publicaciones/spain-28a-a-flicker-in-consumption/
  https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2025/04/28/major-power-outage-spain-portugal-knocks-out-subway-networks-traffic-lights-atms/

## Day 7 — restarting a dead grid ("black start") takes days
- A black start uses special self-starting plants to re-energize the grid island by island; full restoration can take days (large steam/nuclear plants need grid power to restart).
  https://spectrum.ieee.org/power-grid-failure-lights-on
  https://www.windows2universe.org/spaceweather/cold_start.html
- 2003 blackout: most areas restored within ~2 days, parts of Ontario took over a week (Wikipedia, above).
