[calm, intriguing documentary narrator, steady energy] What if you jumped into a tunnel straight through the Earth? [pause] It runs from pole to pole, twelve thousand seven hundred kilometres long, with all the air pumped out. You step off the edge. The deepest hole humans ever dug was twelve kilometres. You pass it in under a minute. And the whole way down, you feel weightless, like an astronaut. Ten minutes in, you are moving faster than a rifle bullet. [building tension] Around you, the rock glows. Five thousand degrees. Twenty-one minutes in, you reach the centre of the Earth, at almost eight kilometres per second. Now gravity pulls you backwards. You slow down, all the way up the other side. Forty-two minutes after you jumped, you rise out of the hole at the opposite pole... and stop, perfectly, right at the edge. [pause] Miss the grab, and you fall all the way back. Forever. Would you take the jump?
