# Sources / numbers (simplified)
- Gravity tunnel through a uniform-density Earth: one-way time ≈ 42.2 min regardless of path (period 84.4 min). Real density profile gives ≈ 38 min (Klotz 2015, Am. J. Phys.).
- Max speed at the centre (uniform Earth) ≈ 7.9 km/s ≈ 28,000 km/h; at t = 10 min speed ≈ 7.9·sin(2π·10/84.4) ≈ 5.4 km/s (rifle bullet ≈ 1 km/s).
- Polar diameter ≈ 12,714 km. Pole-to-pole tunnel avoids Coriolis wall contact.
- Kola Superdeep Borehole: 12.262 km (deepest artificial point). Free fall time to 12 km ≈ √(2·12000/9.8) ≈ 49 s.
- Core temperature ≈ 5,000-5,400 °C.
