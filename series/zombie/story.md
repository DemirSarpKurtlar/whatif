# Zombie Outbreak — interactive series canon 🧟

Protagonist: "you" — a teen in a red beanie and purple hoodie, home alone in a small city (50,000 people).
Format: flat 2D vector style (engine `style: "flat"`, `draw` scenes, see episodes/023-zombie-ep1/draw), 28–35 s,
ends with two choices (A/B). Viewers vote in the comments; the majority decides the next episode.

## Canon so far
- EP1: 11:42 PM, the first bite happens at the city hospital. Infections double every hour.
  You are home alone, the power goes out, sirens everywhere, something scratches at the back door.
- EP1 vote (2026-11-06, 4 voters): A — you hide in the attic.
- EP2: you pull the ladder up into the attic; the back door bursts open; a zombie stands right below the hatch; phone at 7%. They hunt by sound.
- EP2 vote (2026-11-07, 5 voters): A — you stay silent in the attic and wait for dawn.
- EP3: you stay silent all night; the zombie leaves; phone dies at 3:17 AM; at dawn a helicopter announces an evacuation point at the high school stadium, 2 km away. By 6 AM: 64 infected.
- EP3 vote (2026-11-07 post, 1 voter): B — you take the neighbor's bike.
- EP4: 6:35 AM you squeeze through a broken gap in the neighbor's picket fence (bent nail) and ride the bike 2 km to the stadium.
  The rusty chain clicks; they hunt by sound, heads turn. The sun warms them up, so they get faster (new rule: cold = slow, warm = faster).
  At ~1 km left a zombie grabs your sleeve and you rip free (torn cuff). 6:42 AM: 7 hours since the first bite, infected double to 128.
  At the stadium gate: soldiers, a checkpoint sign "NO BITES. NO SCRATCHES." You find a thin red scratch on your wrist — the zombie or the fence?
