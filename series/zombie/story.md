# Zombie Outbreak — interactive series canon 🧟

Protagonist: "you" — a teen in a red beanie and purple hoodie, home alone in a small city (50,000 people).
Format: flat 2D vector style (engine `style: "flat"`, `draw` scenes, see episodes/023-zombie-ep1/draw), 28–35 s,
